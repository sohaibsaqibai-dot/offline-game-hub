❓ FAQ

Good questions

Is Offline Game Hub free?

Yes. It's completely free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no account.

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. Every game, image and sound is packaged inside the extension and made on your device, so once it's installed you can play with Wi-Fi turned off.

How do I install it?

The extension is coming soon to the Chrome Web Store. Until then, you can play every game right here in your browser. The support page also explains how to load the extension manually in Developer mode.

Where are my scores saved?

Only on your own device, in your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded, and you can wipe everything anytime from Settings → Reset all data.

Which browsers are supported?

The extension is built for Google Chrome and works in other Chromium browsers like Microsoft Edge, Brave and Opera. The web version on this site runs in any modern browser.
