Installing the extension

Offline Game Hub is coming soon to the Chrome Web Store. Until it's live, you can play every game in your browser, or load the extension manually:

  1. Download and unzip the extension folder.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top-right switch).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder that contains manifest.json.
  5. Pin the icon to your toolbar and click it to open the Game Hub.

Troubleshooting

I can't hear any sound

Click the 🔊 button in the top bar (or open Settings → Sound effects → On). Browsers only allow sound after your first click or key press, so press Play once and sounds will start.

A game is too big or too small on my screen

Games resize to fit your window. Try the ⛶ fullscreen button in the game bar, or zoom the page with Ctrl + / Ctrl − (Cmd on Mac).

The arrow keys scroll the page instead of moving

Click on the game area once so it has focus, then use the keys. Press Esc anytime to pause.

My scores disappeared

Scores are stored only on your device. Clearing browsing data, using a private window, uninstalling the extension or switching to another computer will start fresh. We can't restore them because we never have a copy.

How do I reset my data?

Open Settings (⚙️) → Your data. You can reset scores, favorites, or everything at once.

Does it work in Edge, Brave or Opera?

Yes. Any Chromium-based browser that supports Chrome extensions can run Offline Game Hub.
